i told you so (or not)

I have to write this down now. In case I need to have a proof when I say I told you so.
Or if it's not happening then for me to reflect on my mistakes.

I'm actually not typing this but I'm speaking in my microphone and my custom built speech-to-text program running on my server is transcribing it into my journaling tool which runs on my web app which I called Cockpit from which I control everything I've built.

Since Fable 5.2, I'm actually quite happy with the results of how the usability feels like.

Now what I wanna say is this:
Surprisingly, it's November now, soon to be Christmas, and I've been what they call AI pilled since end of last year, end of 2025, where most programmers, software developers were ecstatic. I remember I was at Agents Anonymous in Vienna and the vibes were very good. Clawdbot was just exploding in virality. Everyone there was building with agents, but at work I didn't notice this at all.

On the contrary, I ran into resistance at work and I made a few experiences where I did too much agentically and I had to run it back because agents do work differently than people, that conflicts. So I've been doing it the old-fashioned way at work and the agentic way in my personal setups.

Now it's November and companies are starting to ask for more agentic driven stuff, a lot more.
Finally, what was visible in the beginning of the year is happening now and this implies that I'm getting my old-fashioned work automated away.

I guess all of the AI pilled software developers were struggling with those thoughts already (as are mathematicians now lol) and there's a clear answer already present, which is you just change the work you do. You work with agents, you have experience with the stuff the agents do, so you have a taste on what should be done and what shouldn't be done. There's a good book about philosophy of software development which goes into those preferences that agents don't completely get because they're uncertain about the projects they work in. Anyway, I think there's going to be a crash. A wake-up. Something like the old people tell about 2000. Where there was a lot of hype and then the big crash and then it took a long long time to do the build out.

My gut feeling is this will happen again. There is a lot of financialization of services in the Western world. I'm not an expert in this, but there is certainly tension and fear. Especially with state debt being so high. Who knows? I just feel like there will be a reckoning. Also, it seems to me there will be a reckoning because there's so much pressure on everyone to use agents and there's like things you cannot do with agents, at least currently, a lot of tension. And I don't think the agents will be able to do that in the future either because it's not a lack of capabilities or intelligence, but more a matter of integration into existing structures which takes time and is limited by the humans involved (and I do hope we continue to be involved).

I do feel like that hype will explode, will pop, people will be able, will have time to breathe and a new normal will develop. I'm writing this because it seems obvious (or I need to pop my own bubble hereby) and it also seems obvious that the use of agents will just increase after and there will be no going back.

The most interesting thing I think, the next year, will be what models will be available and what the token prices will be. But my gut feeling is that it's going to be cheap going forward to use current state-of-the-art equivalent models. Which means it's gonna be fun once we arrive on the other end.
